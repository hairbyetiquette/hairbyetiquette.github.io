A LITTLE PEACE OF MIND

Privacy policy

A little clarity about your information, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Who we are

This notice covers the Hair by Etiquette website and appointment enquiries. Hair by Etiquette is the contact for information used to arrange your salon visit. Contact us at 26 Old Street, Upton upon Severn, Worcester, WR8 0HW, or call 01684 593920 with a privacy question or request.

Information used through this website

  • Browsing: the web host receives technical information needed to deliver the site, such as an IP address, browser details, requested page and request time. Hosting providers may keep security and access logs.
  • Your privacy choice: when you make a choice, this website stores your Maps and Calendar preferences, policy version and the time of your choice in your browser for up to 180 days. No name or contact information is included.
  • Appointments: if you choose to use Google Calendar, Google displays its booking form. Details you submit, such as your name, email, appointment time and any additional answers requested by the schedule, are sent to Google and made available to the appointment organiser. This website does not operate its own booking database.
  • Telephone enquiries: information you choose to give the salon is used to answer your question or arrange your appointment. Please avoid putting sensitive health information into the online booking form; speak to the salon if you need to discuss a treatment concern.

Why information is used

Appointment information is used to take steps at your request before a booking and to arrange and provide your appointment. Essential website delivery and proportionate security use are based on legitimate interests in providing a working, secure site. Optional embedded Maps and Calendar features are loaded on this website only with your consent. Consent to an embed is separate from the use of information needed to manage a booking.

This website does not include analytics scripts, advertising pixels, newsletter sign-ups, automated profiling or a checkout.

Google Maps and Google Calendar

Both features are off by default and can be allowed separately. When enabled, your browser connects to Google, which can receive technical information such as your IP address, browser/device information and information about your use of the embedded feature. Google may use cookies or similar technologies; its handling can depend on your Google account, browser and privacy settings.

Google describes its own processing, storage, retention and international transfers in its Privacy Policy and cookie information. Data may be processed outside the UK. Please read those notices before using the services. Any booking details you submit are also visible to the schedule organiser so they can manage your appointment.

Links to Google, Facebook, Yell and other external sites take you to services with their own privacy practices. Clicking an external link is your choice to visit that service; declining embeds does not block those links.

How long information is kept

Your browser preference expires after 180 days from your last saved choice and can be changed sooner using Cookie settings. The preference may remain in browser storage until your next visit or until you clear it, but an expired choice is not used to enable Google features.

Appointment information is needed to manage your visit and any relevant follow-up, cancellation or dispute. The period may depend on the booking and any applicable record-keeping duties. Contact the salon to ask about a particular booking or request deletion. Google and any hosting provider control retention of their own service records under their applicable policies.

Your choices and rights

You can use the site and call the salon without allowing Google embeds. Change or withdraw embed permission at any time using . Withdrawing permission removes loaded embeds from this page and prevents them loading on later visits unless you allow them again. It cannot undo information already sent to Google, cancel an appointment or clear cookies stored on Google’s domains; you can manage those through your browser and Google settings.

Depending on the information and legal basis, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or restrict personal information, object to its use, and receive a portable copy. To make a request, contact the salon using the details above. For Google’s own processing, use the controls and contact routes in Google’s privacy notice.

You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

Changes to this notice

We update this page when the website’s features or information practices change. The date below identifies this version. See our Cookie policy for the details of browser storage and optional embeds.
