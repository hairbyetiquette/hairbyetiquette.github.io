A LITTLE PEACE OF MIND

Cookie policy

Your visit. Your choice. A clear guide to what loads in your browser.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

The short version

The core salon website, photos and fonts load from the website itself. Google Maps and Google Calendar stay off until you allow the relevant feature. We do not add analytics, advertising or social-media tracking widgets.

Essential preference storage

We use your browser’s local storage, rather than a first-party cookie, to remember a choice you have made. Storage used to remember privacy choices is treated as essential to honouring those choices. It is not used to identify you across websites.

Item Purpose Duration
hbe-privacy-choices-v1 Stores your separate Maps and Calendar choices, the policy version and the time saved. Choice honoured for up to 180 days. An expired entry is removed when read on a later visit.

If storage is blocked or unavailable, your choice applies only to the current page and you may be asked again when you navigate or return.

Optional Google features

Choice What it enables Who provides it
Google Maps An interactive map displayed inline in the Find us section. It appears automatically after you allow Google Maps. Google
Google Calendar The appointment scheduling page inside this website. Google

Allowing one does not allow the other. Loading either feature connects your browser to Google, shares technical details such as your IP address, and may allow cookies or similar storage. The names, uses and lifetimes of Google’s cookies can vary with your account, region and settings. See Google’s cookie information and Privacy Policy for its current details.

How to make or change a choice

Use the banner to accept optional features, reject them or choose individual settings. You can also allow a single feature from its placeholder. Nothing is preselected for a first-time visitor.

is available in every page footer. Switch off either feature and save to remove its embed. Rejecting optional features leaves the salon information, photos, gallery and phone links available.

Withdrawing permission stops future embedded loads; it cannot retrieve data already sent or remove Google-domain cookies. You can delete cookies and site data through your browser, and manage Google information through Google’s privacy controls. A saved choice expires after 180 days, after which we ask again.

External links and booking

You can choose to open Google Maps or the booking page in a separate tab without enabling an embed here. That visit is governed by Google’s own settings and notices. A phone booking is also available on 01684 593920.

Online bookings must be made at least 48 hours in advance. For an earlier appointment, call the salon and we’ll see if we have a slot.
