Before you settle in

Not sure which service to choose?

A consultation is a lovely place to start. Bring your ideas, your questions, or simply an idea of how you’d like your hair to feel.

Thinking about a colour change?

Start with a colour consultation to talk through your current colour and your goals. Confirm any patch-test requirements with the salon before a colour appointment.

Can I book an appointment here?

Yes. Choose an available time using our Google Calendar booking page below, then follow the steps to complete your booking. Prefer to speak to us? Call 01684 593920. Online bookings need at least 48 hours’ notice. Call us to ask about earlier appointments.
